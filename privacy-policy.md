# Privacy Policy for "轉職白板題"

Effective date: November 18, 2026

"轉職白板題" ("the App") is a learning app for practising algorithm problems by arranging blocks of code. The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

- No account is required, and the App never asks for personal information.
- The App itself makes no network connections. No data leaves your device through the App.
- The App contains no ads and no third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
- The App does not request access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photos.

The App stores your learning progress (cleared levels, stars, hints used, practice time, review list) and your settings (code font size, sound effects, background music, whether the tutorial has been seen) on your device only. We cannot access this data. iCloud sync is not used; if you have device backups turned on, this data may be included in your own backup, which is handled by Apple.

The "Licenses and sources" page under Settings contains two links (the dataset source and the full license text). They open in your browser only when you tap them; the destination sites have their own privacy policies.

To erase your data, delete the App from your device. The App currently has no separate "reset progress" option.

The App is not directed at children. Because it collects no data from anyone, it collects no personal data from children.

If a future version changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated first, with a new effective date.
